- Subtle gradient: navy to near-black across the page

- Full-width hero with left-aligned text and floating code-snippet decoration
- Bento-grid project layout — asymmetric card sizes

- Subtle dot-grid background pattern (very low opacity)

## Selected Approach: **"Midnight Craft"** (Approach 2)

Deep navy-black background (`#0A0F1E`), cyan accent (`#22D3EE`), Syne display font, bento-grid projects, glowing interactions, dot-grid background texture. This approach is professional, technical, and memorable — it matches the caliber of Joh's resume without feeling like a template.
